# pinetree wiki

Status: living documentation, refreshed 2026-10-20 (freshness pass). Phase 3 is complete. Phase 4 (many servers working as one) has not started.
The authoritative sources are `sns-server-proposal.md` (product and architecture) and the frozen contracts in `docs/`. This wiki is the plain-language overview. If they disagree, the proposal and the contracts win.
