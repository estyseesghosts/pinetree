## DIVERGENCE / behavior notes (all recorded in the manifest)

- `i/apps` `lastUsedAt` never tracked (would double hot-path store writes).
- `i/authorized-apps` one row per grant (no app registry to dedupe by).
- `change-password`/`regenerate-token` wrong-password → 401
  `AUTHENTICATION_FAILED` (upstream throws untyped → generic 500).
- `upload-from-url` sync + packed return; `url-preview` successes-only cache.
- `lastUsedAt` sorts fall back to creation order.
