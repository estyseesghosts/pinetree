## Key adaptation (content-addressed blobs)

birchtree overwrites `file_key` bytes in place; pinetree's `BlobStore`
is SHA-256 CAS, so derived bytes land under their own key and the row
is re-committed (same id) pointing at the new key. Read-back verified
before commit; old bytes stay while referenced (dedup sharers keep
working); unreferenced bytes are reconciler-sweep candidates.
`thumbnailUrl` = file's own `url` until a thumbnail route exists;
preview bytes stored CAS for the future route.
